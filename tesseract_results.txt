Propeller Drawing-Out and Fitting P- 2
2. Procedure of drawing-out for propeller
2-1 _Lift the propeller using wire, chain block and winch.
2-2 Take off the fair water cap.
2-3 Loose the propeller nut using the turning bars for propeller nut.
2-4 Fit the wooden piece for buffer between the propeller nut and propeller boss.
At this time, a clearance between the wooden piece and the aft end surface of the propeller boss
shall be about 20 mm. ( Corresponding to the maximum fitting travel
 
)
2-5 Take off the plug from the boss expansion oil port in the propeller boss and fit the coupler
with packing to oil port in the propeller boss.
2-6 Install the flexible hoses between the oil pump and oil port in the propeller boss.
2-7 Oil pressure of oil pump is raised gradually until about 58 MPa.
In case of propeller boss not move, the oil pressure is raised gradually.
( In this case, the pressure must not be exceeded 70 MPa. )
2-8 After successful movement of propeller boss aftwards , stop the oil pump
 
, i.e. oil pump indication 0 MPa.
2-9 Take off the coupler with packing from oil port in the propeller boss.
2-10 Take off the propeller nut and the wooden piece.
2-11 Remove the propeller using wire, chain block and winch.

Propeller Drawing-Out and Fitting P- 3
3. Preparation of fitting for propeller
3-1 Clean the cone part of the propeller boss and propeller shaft with thinner.
3-2 Lift up the propeller and mount it on the propeller shaft.
At this time, position the No.1 cyl. top mark of the propeller boss end to the aft end of cone part
of propeller shaft.
3-3 Fit the propeller nut (hydraulic type) to the propeller shaft.
3-4 Fit the coupler with packing to the boss expansion oil port in the propeller boss, hydraulic nut
and 2 way branch with shut off valve.
3-5 Fill oil in the oil pump.
3-6 Install the flexible hoses between the 2 way branch and hydraulic nut, between 2 way branch and
expansion oil port in propeller boss and between 2 way branch and the oil pump.
3-7 Fit the dial gauges with the magnetic holder on the propeller shaft.
3-8 Fit a thermometer to each of the propeller boss and propeller shaft end.

Decision of travel of the propeller boss
Just before the propeller fitting, the temperature of the propeller boss and propeller shaft should
be measured. The required fitting travel to be decided based on measured fitting temperature
according to “ CALCULATION SHEET OF PROPELLER FITTING “ ( Fig.3
 
) .
Dry fitting ( First step )
The oil pressure of hydraulic nut to be increased to rule required starting point load ;
Rule required starting point load = 6.000 x10°N
The oil pressure of hydraulic nut= 4.40 MPa (Nutram area= 136,345 mm )
In this case, do not fill the lubricating oil to the interface of propeller boss and shaft.
The dial gauge to be fitted at fwd end of propeller boss and set at " ZERO " reading.
The oil pressure of hydraulic nut to be raised gradually and the travel of boss to be measured
by the dial gauge at oil pressure 10, 15, 20, 25, 30 MPa.
Wet fitting (Second step)
Fill the lubricating oil to interface of propeller boss and shaft.
The oil pressure of hydraulic nut and the interface of propeller boss and shaft to be raised gradually
The travel of boss to be measured at oil pressure 10, 15, 20, 25, 30, ...... MPa.
( The oil pressure of the interfaces must not exceed 70 MPa. )
After these measured points are confirmed being on a line, the oil pressure to be raised until
the required fitting travel of boss is obtained.
When the propeller boss to be reached in the required fitting travel, release the boss expansion
oil pressure and then after about 2~3 minutes the fitting oil pressure.
Remove the dial gauge, flexible hoses and pump etc.
Fit the plug to the boss expansion oil port in the propeller boss and hydraulic nut.
